# LinkedIn

**Summary**: A collection of notable LinkedIn posts saved for reference — career resources, job-search platforms, CV/cover-letter guidance, and industry skill trends, mostly related to GIS/geospatial careers and UN/international-development jobs.
**Last updated**: 2026-08-25

---

- [10 In-Demand GIS Skills for 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7482456210810683392/): LinkedIn infographic post by Mashford Mahute arguing that GIS careers now require more than cartography — automation, AI/ML modeling, satellite imagery analysis, web app development, cloud infrastructure, and turning geospatial data into business intelligence. Recommends building skills one at a time rather than mastering everything at once. GIS careers, GeoAI, geospatial skills, cloud GIS, automation, 2026 trends [[Remote_Sensing]] [[Machine_Learning]] [[Python]] [[ArcGIS]] [[QGIS]]

- [How to Write a Cover Letter for United Nations Opportunities](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7479592977909280768/): Guide from Scholarships Corner (LinkedIn) on writing UN cover letters, based on official OHRM and UNDP guidance, including a sample UN cover letter format. Independent educational guide, not affiliated with the UN. UN careers, cover letter, OHRM, UNDP, job applications

- [A Beginner's Step-by-Step Guide to Creating Your UN Inspira Account](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7479392330794512384/): Nine-step walkthrough by Nelly Kerubo for setting up and using Inspira, the UN Secretariat's official recruitment platform — covers account registration, email verification, building a Personal History Profile (PHP), uploading supporting documents, searching vacancies, tailoring applications, and tracking application status. UN Inspira, UN recruitment, Personal History Profile, job applications, UN careers

- [Best Platforms to Search for UN & International NGO Jobs](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7479382951215095808/): Roundup by Nelly Kerubo of platforms for UN, NGO, and international-development job seekers — UN Careers, UN Talent, ReliefWeb Jobs, Impactpool, Devex, and UN Volunteers — plus a reminder to check individual agency career pages (UNDP, UNICEF, UNHCR, WHO, WFP, IOM, IFRC, Save the Children, World Vision, CARE, Mercy Corps) since vacancies are often posted exclusively there. UN jobs, NGO jobs, ReliefWeb, Impactpool, Devex, job search platforms

- [The Exact CV Structure That Gets Shortlisted for UN and INGO Roles](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7477218581319958528/): Mujahid Khan's recommended section order and formatting rules for international-development CVs — contact info (no photo), professional summary, education before experience for UN technical roles, reverse-chronological experience, ATS-friendly keyword/skills section, certifications, languages, and publications/field reports; plus font (Calibri/Garamond 11pt), 1-inch margins, no tables/columns, .docx format, 2-3 page length. UN CV, INGO careers, resume format, ATS, job applications
